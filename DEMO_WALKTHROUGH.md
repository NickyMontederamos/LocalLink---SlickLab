# LocalLink Full Demo Walkthrough

## Fastest route

1. Open the HTML file.
2. Select **Demo Tour** in the top navigation or **Start Full Walkthrough** in the hero.
3. Use **Next**, **Back**, or the left and right arrow keys. Press **Escape** to exit.

## Manual demonstration script

### 1. Product promise
Explain that LocalLink is not a freelancer directory. It is the managed layer between client demand and accountable service delivery.

### 2. Ecosystem
Hover over and click Demand, Supply, Trust, Capital, Partners, Agent Layer, and LocalLink Core. Explain how the platform coordinates each relationship.

### 3. Transaction workflow
Click the 12 workflow stages in sequence. Completed stages turn green and generate audit events. Reloading the page retains completed stages in the same browser.

### 4. Form validation
Go to Job Intelligence and intentionally test invalid input:

- Empty request
- Request shorter than 30 characters
- Budget below PHP 1,000
- Budget not divisible by PHP 500
- Empty or invalid location
- Unchecked safe-data confirmation

Submit the form. The summary identifies invalid fields and focus moves to the first issue.

### 5. Successful intake
Click **Load demo request**, then **Generate structured brief**. Review the generated JSON-style brief, estimated pricing range, governance flags, and candidate cards.

### 6. Candidate explanation
Select **Review explanation** under any talent. Explain that production scoring must use verified evidence, versioned weights, monitoring, and human review.

### 7. Architecture
Show the Experience, Domain, Control, Intelligence, and Evidence layers. Emphasize:

- Database equals factual state
- Policy equals authorization
- AI equals recommendation
- Humans handle consequential exceptions

### 8. Economics
Move the project-value and talent-share sliders. Explain that the output is illustrative and category economics should not be permanently hard-coded.

### 9. Audit trail
Open **Audit Trail** to display actions recorded during the demonstration.

### 10. Utilities
Test the contrast toggle, Share or Copy Link, Print, and Back to Top controls.

## Production boundary

The HTML is a product and interaction prototype. Real authentication, payments, messaging, talent verification, dispute handling, and persistent audit logging require secure backend implementation.
