SEO-ready content
What is LocalLink?

LocalLink is a managed service marketplace. It structures client requirements, recommends qualified talent, manages scope and milestones, records payment state, and supports delivery verification.

How is LocalLink different from a freelancer directory?

A directory leaves discovery and transaction management to the customer. LocalLink is designed to coordinate intake, matching, scoping, milestones, acceptance, disputes, and reputation as one accountable workflow.

Does AI make payment or dispute decisions?

No. AI provides recommendations and risk indicators. Database state, server-side policy, and authorized human review control consequential actions such as payout, refund, and serious dispute resolution.

How are talents ranked?

The proposed Talent Fit Score considers skill relevance, experience, reliability, quality history, budget fit, availability, and communication. Recommendations should expose reason codes and verified evidence.
